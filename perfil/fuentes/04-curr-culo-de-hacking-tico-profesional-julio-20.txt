### KEYWORDS: Ethical Hacking, Malware Analysis, Social Engineering, Mobile Hacking, Active Directory Hacking ###

Resumen: This document outlines a comprehensive **curriculum for professional ethical hacking**, structured as a series of progressive modules delivered via an online learning platform. The training begins with **foundational environment setup** and reconnaissance, moving systematically through the **standard phases of a cyberattack**, including scanning, enumeration, and exploitation. Students explore specialized technical domains such as **Active Directory security**, mobile platforms, and web application vulnerabilities, alongside deceptive tactics like **social engineering**. Ultimately, the course emphasizes the **methodical reporting of findings**, ensuring that practitioners can effectively communicate risks to improve overall digital defense.
